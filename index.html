Working notes

I'm a web developer and 3D artist based in Calgary, focused on building modern, interactive digital experiences. I make responsive sites and immersive 3D on the web with Three.js, and build architectural visualizations in Blender and Unreal Engine 5.

I'm drawn to the intersection of engineering, architecture, and 3D visualization — building things that are as technically solid as they are visually striking. Alongside the build work I hold an Advanced SEO certification and keep sites fast, structured, and easy to find.

Outside of tech, music has shaped a lot of who I am: six years of guitar, 300+ hours with a church band and 1,000+ hours of personal practice, plus double bass across jazz, rock, classical, and flamenco.

This page is the index — one place that ties the separate builds together. The studio work lives under SIMAX.
